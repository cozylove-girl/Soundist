# Moodist upstream third-party asset declaration

Source: <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/remvze/moodist/main/README.md>

Repository commit used for the packaged files: `983f7412e8cd054e76d156977c563da2028e4428`

The upstream README states that some sounds are sourced from third-party providers and are subject to different licenses. It names these two license families:

- Pixabay Content License: <https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/>
- CC0 1.0: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>

The upstream repository does not provide a per-file mapping between those license families and the 84 files in `public/sounds/`.
