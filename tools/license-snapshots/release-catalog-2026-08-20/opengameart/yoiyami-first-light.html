First Light Particles is a calm, atmospheric piano piece designed for story-driven games, exploration scenes, dream sequences, and emotional cutscenes.

It features:

•soft shimmering piano

•subtle low-frequency pulses

•transparent blue-tone harmony

•slow emotional movement

•no groove, no percussion

•gentle ambient pads drifting like early morning light

 

The mood is peaceful, introspective, and slightly cosmic, suitable for:

•VN / visual novels

•indie RPGs

•space exploration

•atmospheric platformers

•menu / title screen

•meditation / quiet areas

•“first morning light”, “awakening”, “memories resurfacing” scenes
