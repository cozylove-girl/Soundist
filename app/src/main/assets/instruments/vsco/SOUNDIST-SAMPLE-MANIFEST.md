# Soundist VSCO 2 CE Sample Subset

Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE

License: CC0 1.0. The upstream license text is preserved in
`VSCO-2-CE-LICENSE.txt`.

This folder contains the 15-sample subset packaged by the Soundist Android
generative-radio engine:

- Upright piano: C3, C4, C5
- Harp: C3, D4, C5
- Flute: C3, C4, C5
- Violin section sustain vibrato: A3, C4, B4
- Cello section sustain vibrato: D2, C3, B3

The files were copied without audio modification. Their packaged paths, byte
sizes, and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
`../../../../../../VSCO_SAMPLE_LICENSE_MANIFEST.tsv`. Loudness calibration
is maintained separately by the audio engine and does not change the CC0
status of these source samples.
